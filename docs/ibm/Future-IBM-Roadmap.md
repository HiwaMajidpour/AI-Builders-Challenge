# Future IBM Roadmap

> **Project:** AI Creative Studio
> **Challenge:** IBM AI Builders Challenge 2026
> **Version:** 1.0

---

# Overview

AI Creative Studio has been architected with a long-term vision of evolving from a frontend AI demonstration into a production-ready enterprise AI platform powered by IBM technologies.

This roadmap outlines the planned evolution of the platform over multiple development phases while maintaining compatibility with IBM Granite Foundation Models and the IBM watsonx ecosystem.

---

# Vision

The long-term vision is to build an intelligent enterprise workspace that combines AI-assisted content creation, collaboration, knowledge management, and workflow automation within the IBM Cloud ecosystem.
