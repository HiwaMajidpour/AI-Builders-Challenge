# Overview

IBM Granite Foundation Models serve as the intelligence layer of AI Creative Studio.

The platform leverages Granite to assist users in generating high-quality content, improving productivity, and supporting enterprise AI workflows.

This document describes both the current implementation and the future enterprise capabilities enabled by IBM Granite.

---

# Objectives

The primary objectives of Granite integration are to:

- Accelerate content creation
- Improve writing quality
- Reduce repetitive work
- Support creative workflows
- Enable enterprise AI adoption
- Demonstrate practical IBM AI usage
